# Vanya Arc — brand and trademark policy

**Effective for current and future releases from 22 November 2026.** Copyright © 2026 Vanya Afanasov **to the extent of rights actually owned or controlled**. This policy distinguishes official identity from the open-source components. It is not a trademark registration certificate or a claim that a registration search has been completed.

## Official identity

The name **Vanya Arc**, the wordmark **Vanya Arc / UI**, the distinctive continuous two-crest **V → A** symbol, official lockups and any official brand artwork are identifiers of the Vanya Arc project family. The small first dark crest flows into a larger warm crest. Preserve the original artwork; do not redraw it as a generic sine wave.

Source of official artwork: [src/brand/VanyaArcArtwork.ts](src/brand/VanyaArcArtwork.ts), [public/vanya-arc-mark.svg](public/vanya-arc-mark.svg), and the [brand manual](docs/identity/brand.md). Branding applications are different from `@vanya/ui` React primitives.

## What you may do without asking

- Mention **Vanya Arc** and **Vanya Arc / UI** accurately in articles, documentation, tutorials, comparisons, compatibility notices and links.
- Display the **unchanged** official logo where it identifies or discusses the official project; don't imply that Vanya Arc sponsors or endorses the surrounding product.
- Include the unchanged logo when distributing an unchanged official version on otherwise applicable terms.
- Exercise uses that applicable law permits without permission (including applicable descriptive or nominative references).

## Ask first / not authorized by this policy

- Use the official symbol or wordmark as the logo of a different application, company, fork, service, product, merchandise item or paid offering.
- Make a modified logo that suggests it is an official Vanya Arc product, or imply partnership, sponsorship or certification.
- Register a confusingly similar mark/domain, or distribute brand artwork as a standalone stock-graphics asset.

For permission requests, use [the public project](https://github.com/VanDalkvist/vanya-ui) or [Vanya Arc](https://vanya-arc.dev). Permission requests can be evaluated individually; this document is not a blanket licensing offer for unrelated commercial branding.

## Software and copyright separation

- The reusable React code, including `src/components/VanyaArcMark.tsx` as a **renderer**, remains MIT.
- The particular illustrated sign path is in `src/brand/VanyaArcArtwork.ts` and the favicon SVG. It is marked `LicenseRef-VanyaArc-Brand`, a reserved-rights / limited-use notice, **not** an OSI-approved open-source license.
- Users of the UI component library can remove or replace the branded mark in forks. Nothing in this policy limits the right to use independently developed alternative icons, styles or brand names.
- The word **Vanya Arc** itself is not asserted to be copyright-protected as a short phrase; its possible role as a source identifier is a distinct trademark question.
- **Previously released MIT versions:** this prospective policy does not undo MIT permissions already given for earlier releases/copies. This is especially important because previous versions embedded the artwork path directly in MIT-licensed code.

## Status and jurisdiction

No assertion is made here that **Vanya Arc** or the V→A symbol is a **registered** trademark; do not use `®` unless a matching registration is verified. `™` may be appropriate as an unregistered-brand signal in some jurisdictions, but is not registration.

Protection of a logo as copyrightable artwork depends on originality under applicable law; a simplified curve or AI-assisted design may not qualify for exclusive copyright protection everywhere. Publication of this policy alone does not create exclusivity. If protection of the mark matters commercially, confirm design provenance and undertake trademark clearance and filing in intended markets.
